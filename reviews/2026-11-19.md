### OpenAI conditions

The [Astra](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.2-astra), [Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.2-sol), [pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) and [changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog) pages confirm the November 17 release and current tariffs. Current prices are recorded from the November 19 review rather than asserting an unverified historical rate.

Above 272,000 single-request input tokens, including cache, input/cache prices double and output is 1.5x for the entire request. Batch is 0.5x and Fast 2x at the applicable context length. Ultrafast is 3x through February 28, 2027 Pacific time and 6x from March 1 Pacific. The date-only lookup intentionally leaves March 1 unpriced to avoid assigning a tariff across an unresolved timezone boundary; 6x rows begin March 2. This is conservative missing coverage, not a claim that the promotion lasts longer.

Daily and multi-request session totals cannot establish an actual request context tier. For Codex, the default API-equivalent valuation now explicitly uses ordinary-context Standard rates, rather than leaving the whole model unpriced. This is a reference-value policy, not a claim about measured context length or an actual bill. It applies from the November 17 model launch using the current documented rate. The full qualified OpenAI tariffs remain unchanged; the preceding client tariffs remain accessible under explicit `standard-short-context` and `standard-long-context` bands. Other service tiers, regional uplifts and cache-write TTLs are not inferred. The immutable `2026.11.19.1` release retains the earlier aggregate-unpriced policy; this revision changes the default client reference, not the provider tariff or Token counts.
